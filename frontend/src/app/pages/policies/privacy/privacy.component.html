Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how EventHub collects, protects, uses, and handles your personal information.

૧. અમે કઈ માહિતી એકત્રિત કરીએ છીએ (1. Information We Collect)

EventHub (“we”, “our”) collects information that you directly provide when registering an account, booking tickets, posting reviews, or submitting support queries:

  • Personal Identifiers: Full Name, Email Address, Phone/Mobile Number.
  • Academic/Profile Details: Department, College/Institution Name, Academic Year (for student events and eligibility verification).
  • Transactional Metadata: Event details, quantity of tickets, Booking ID (`TRX-...`), payment timestamps, and transaction reference IDs issued by our payment gateway.
  • User Content: Feedback ratings, event inquiries, and public event comments.

૨. માહિતીનો ઉપયોગ કેવી રીતે થાય છે (2. How We Use Your Information)

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To generate, deliver, and validate your official E-Tickets and Booking QR codes.
  • To transmit booking confirmations, event schedule updates, and urgent alerts.
  • To authenticate user sessions securely via JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • To provide customer support and address queries submitted to gondaliyakishan839@gmail.com.
  • To prevent fraudulent bookings and maintain the security of our platform.

૩. ચુકવણી સુરક્ષા અને Razorpay (3. Payment Security & Razorpay Compliance)

Security Guarantee: We prioritize your financial data privacy with bank-grade standards:

  • All online financial transactions are encrypted using 256-bit SSL encryption and processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment aggregator.
  • No Storage of Sensitive Card/UPI Data: EventHub never stores or has access to your full credit/debit card numbers, CVV, Net Banking passwords, or UPI PINs.
  • All payment requests and webhooks follow cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature verification.

૪. ડેટા શેરિંગ નીતિ (4. Data Sharing & Third Parties)

We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers.

Your data is shared strictly in the following limited circumstances:

  • Event Organizers: Organizers receive attendee name, email, and ticket status solely for venue check-in verification and event administration.
  • Payment Processors: Necessary transaction data is shared securely with Razorpay and banking networks to complete payment processing and refunds.
  • Legal Obligations: When strictly required by law enforcement or regulatory authorities under Indian law.

૫. કૂકીઝ અને સ્થાનિક સંગ્રહ (5. Cookies & Local Storage)

EventHub utilizes browser `localStorage` solely to maintain your active login session (JWT authentication token) so you do not have to re-login on every page navigation. We do not use third-party tracking cookies for cross-site behavioral advertising.

૬. ડેટા સુરક્ષા અને વપરાશકર્તાના અધિકારો (6. Data Retention & User Rights)

  • Access & Update: You may review and update your profile information at any time under your Account Profile.
  • Comment & Booking Management: You have the right to edit or delete comments you have posted on event pages.
  • Account Deletion: You may request complete erasure of your personal data by contacting us via email.
