Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy details how EventHub collects, protects, processes, and safeguards your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

EventHub collects personal information provided directly by users during account registration, ticket booking, event hosting, or support inquiries:

  • Personal Details: Full Name, Email Address, and Phone/Mobile Number.
  • Organizer Profiles: Organization/Brand Name, Event venue details, and contact coordinates for event moderation.
  • Transaction Records: Booking references (`TRX-...`), quantity of tickets, timestamps, and payment transaction tokens generated by our gateway.
  • Communications & Reviews: Public ratings, event feedback, and customer support messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • To generate, deliver, and validate official e-tickets and venue check-in QR codes.
  • To send order confirmations, booking receipts, event schedule reminders, and urgent organizer notices.
  • To authenticate and manage secure user login sessions using JSON Web Tokens (JWT).
  • To review and moderate organizer event submissions before publishing.
  • To provide customer support and address inquiries sent to gondaliyakishan839@gmail.com.

3. Payment Security & Razorpay Compliance

Bank-Grade Financial Protection:

  • All financial transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL encryption and processed through Razorpay, a certified PCI-DSS Level 1 payment aggregator.
  • Zero Storage of Sensitive Card/Banking Data: EventHub never stores or has access to your full credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, Net Banking passwords, or UPI PINs.
  • All payment notifications and callbacks are verified via HMAC-SHA256 digital cryptographic signatures.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers.

Data is shared solely under the following limited operational circumstances:

  • Event Organizers: Organizers receive attendee names and ticket verification codes strictly for venue admission control.
  • Payment Processors: Necessary payment data is shared securely with Razorpay and banking networks to execute transactions and process authorized refunds.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by applicable Indian law or official court order.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

EventHub uses browser `localStorage` solely to maintain your active authentication session (JWT token). We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-site tracking cookies.

6. Data Retention & User Rights

  • Profile Management: You can edit your personal information at any time in your Account Profile.
  • Data Removal: You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting our support team via email.
