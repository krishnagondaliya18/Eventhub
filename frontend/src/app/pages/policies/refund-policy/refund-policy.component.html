૭ દિવસની રિફંડ ગેરંટી (7-Day Refund Policy)

You are eligible for a refund if a cancellation request is made up to 7 days prior to the scheduled event date, or in any instance where an event is cancelled by the organizer.

૧. વપરાશકર્તા દ્વારા ટિકિટ રદ કરવાની નીતિ (1. User Ticket Cancellation - 7 Days)

We understand that schedules and travel plans can change unexpectedly. EventHub offers a structured cancellation policy:

  • 7+ Days Before Event: If you cancel your confirmed ticket booking at least 7 days prior to the scheduled event date/time, you will receive a refund of the ticket base price (minus standard non-refundable payment gateway transaction charges where applicable).
  • Less Than 7 Days: Cancellations requested within less than 7 days of the scheduled event date or after the event has commenced are strictly non-refundable, as ticket seats and venue resources are already locked by the organizer.

૨. આયોજક દ્વારા ઇવેન્ટ રદ થવાની સ્થિતિમાં ૧૦૦% રિફંડ (2. Event Cancellation by Organizer)

If an event is cancelled, indefinitely postponed, or rescheduled to a date you cannot attend by the event organizer:

  • 100% Full Refund: You are entitled to a full 100% refund of the total ticket amount paid.
  • Automatic Processing: Refunds for cancelled events will be initiated automatically without requiring manual follow-ups.

૩. રિફંડ પ્રોસેસિંગ સમયમર્યાદા (3. Refund Processing Timeline - 5 to 7 Days)

Once your cancellation request is approved or an event is cancelled:

  • Processing Window: Refunds are processed and credited to your original payment source (UPI account, Bank Account, Credit/Debit Card) within 5 to 7 business/working days.
  • Payment Mode: The refund is credited directly via our payment aggregator Razorpay back to the exact bank account or UPI VPA from which the transaction was originally executed.
  • Confirmation Alert: You will receive an email confirmation containing the refund reference and transaction identifier.

૪. રિફંડ મેળવવાની સરળ પ્રક્રિયા (4. How to Request a Cancellation / Refund)

You can request a cancellation using any of the following convenient methods:

  1. Via EventHub Portal: Log in to your account → Go to My Bookings → Locate your booking → Click Cancel Booking.
  2. Via Email Support: Send an email to gondaliyakishan839@gmail.com from your registered email address with your Booking ID (`TRX-...`) and event title.
  3. Via Queries Page: Submit an online inquiry through our Support & Queries portal.

૫. નોન-રિફંડેબલ પરિસ્થિતિઓ (5. Non-Refundable Scenarios)

  • Failure to attend the event (No-show) on the scheduled date and time.
  • Denial of entry by venue security due to violation of venue rules, misconduct, intoxication, or failure to present valid government photo ID.
  • Free registration tickets (no monetary transaction occurred).
