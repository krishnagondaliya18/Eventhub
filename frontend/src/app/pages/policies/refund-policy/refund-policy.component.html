Guaranteed 7-Day Refund Policy

You are eligible for a ticket refund if a cancellation request is submitted at least 7 days prior to the scheduled event date, or in any instance where an event is cancelled by the organizer.

1. Attendee Ticket Cancellation (7-Day Policy)

We understand that travel and personal schedules can change unexpectedly. EventHub offers a clear and structured cancellation policy:

  • 7+ Days Before Event: If you cancel your confirmed ticket booking at least 7 days prior to the scheduled event date/time, you will receive a refund of the ticket price (minus standard payment gateway transaction fees where applicable).
  • Less Than 7 Days: Cancellations requested within less than 7 days of the scheduled event date or after the event has started are strictly non-refundable, as ticket allocations and venue logistics are finalized by the organizer.

2. 100% Refund on Event Cancellation by Organizer

If an event is cancelled, indefinitely postponed, or rescheduled to a date you cannot attend:

  • 100% Full Refund: You will automatically receive a 100% full refund of the total ticket amount paid.
  • Automatic Refund Initiation: Refunds for cancelled events will be processed automatically without requiring manual user claims.

3. Refund Processing Timeline (5 to 7 Business Days)

Once your cancellation request is verified or an event cancellation is announced:

  • Processing Window: Refunds are credited back to your original payment source (UPI account, Bank Account, or Credit/Debit Card) within 5 to 7 business/working days.
  • Direct Source Settlement: The refund is credited directly through our payment partner Razorpay back to the exact bank account or UPI VPA from which the transaction originated.
  • Email Confirmation: You will receive an automated refund confirmation email containing the refund ARN / transaction ID.

4. How to Request a Ticket Cancellation

You can request a cancellation through any of the following convenient methods:

  1. Via EventHub Portal: Log in to your account → Go to My Bookings → Find your booking → Click Cancel Booking.
  2. Via Support Email: Send an email to gondaliyakishan839@gmail.com from your registered email with your Booking ID (`TRX-...`) and event name.
  3. Via Support Desk: Submit an online inquiry through our Support & Queries portal.

5. Non-Refundable Scenarios

  • Failure to attend the event (No-show) on the scheduled event date and time.
  • Denial of venue entry due to misconduct, failure to present government photo ID, or violation of organizer venue rules.
  • Free registration tickets (where no monetary payment occurred).
